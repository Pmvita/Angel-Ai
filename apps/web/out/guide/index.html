AngelAi

Guide

How to use AngelAi for interviews and meetings.

1. Start listening

Open the desk and click the mic button to start a session. Speech is captured via your browser/Electron mic and streamed into the transcript.

2. Vision analysis

While listening in the desktop app, screenshots are captured every few seconds and analyzed by your AI provider. Results show summary, key points, action items, and detected text.

3. Ask questions

Type a question in the Ask tab. The AI answers using the live transcript as context. Supports OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, LM Studio, and custom endpoints.

4. Notes

Add manual notes or let auto-notes capture highlights. Pin important ones; export or email them later.

5. Translation

Enable real-time translation in Settings to translate each transcript segment to your target language.

6. Export

Export sessions as Markdown, PDF, TXT, or JSON, or email them directly from the footer.

7. Kill switch

Arm the kill switch to instantly hide the overlay and stop capture. Toggle from Settings or the tray icon (`Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+S`).

8. Zoom / screen-share caveat

The overlay is a visible window. It is NOT hidden from screen capture. If an interviewer asks you to share your screen, hide the overlay first (kill switch or tray) or move it to a second monitor not being shared. Voice-only / camera-only interviews are safe.
